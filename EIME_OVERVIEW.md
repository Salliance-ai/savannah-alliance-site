## 💡 Key Innovations

### 🧠 **Emotional Intelligence Layer**
Every interaction is analyzed for:
- Sentiment (positive/negative)
- Primary emotion (anxiety, trust, curiosity, etc.)
- Trust signals vs. anxiety markers
- Emotional stability over time

### 🎯 **Trust-Based Adaptation**
AI intensity adapts based on trust score:
- < 60: Minimal AI, human-first messaging
- 60-80: Moderate AI, balanced approach
- > 80: Full AI, personalized experience

### 🔄 **Therapist Framework Applied**
Marketing operates like therapy:
- Listen first (data collection)
- Validate concerns (acknowledge emotions)
- Empower users (give control)
- Educate clearly (transparent explanations)
- Evolve continuously (learn from feedback)
