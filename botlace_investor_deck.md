### Slide 1 — Botlace: AI Operating Layer for Healthcare Delivery
- Unifies ClinicalOps, RevenueOps, and MarketOps under one adaptive AI layer
- Built for scale, compliance, and continuous learning (FHIR-native, HIPAA-ready)
- Wedge: behavioral health and multi-site specialty clinics

### Slide 2 — Problem
- Fragmented stack: GHL/marketing, scheduling, EHR, billing—no end-to-end visibility
- Manual documentation + coding → low productivity, revenue leakage
- Compliance risk and audit burden; leadership lacks real-time KPIs

### Slide 3 — Solution
- ACRE: medical-grade AI layer for reasoning, documentation, and decision support
- Data & Integration Mesh: secure FHIR hub across Epic, ICANotes, Tebra, GHL
- SaaS Fabric: multitenant, zero-touch provisioning, modular modules

### Slide 4 — Product Modules
- ClinicalOps AI: real-time notes, ICD/CPT assist, treatment plans
- CareNav & Smart Scheduling: predictive fill, triage assistant, routing
- Patient360 Hub: unified clinical + engagement profile
- GHL-Powered MarketOps CRM: AI scoring, outreach, funnel analytics
- RevenueOps: anomaly detection, forecasting; QB/Stripe/Athena-ready

### Slide 5 — Architecture (Reference)
- ACRE: FHIR-aware RAG, rules+LLM verification, explainability & audit logs
- Integration Mesh: FHIR store, event bus, connector hub, de-ID/PHI guard
- SaaS Fabric: multitenancy, RBAC/SSO/SCIM, usage-based billing (Stripe)

### Slide 6 — Compliance & Security
- HIPAA-grade: encryption, egress allowlisting, private subnets, BAAs
- PHI governance: inline DLP, least-privilege prompts, de-ID to lakehouse
- Audit-ready: immutable logs, model prompts/outputs with retention policies

### Slide 7 — Business Model & Pricing
- Starter $499: up to 5 providers; GHL CRM; ClinicalOps + scheduling automation
- Practice $1,999: up to 25 providers; full MarketOps AI; engagement analytics
- Enterprise $15k+ usage: Epic integration, federated learning, multi-site controls
- Usage drivers: notes generated, appointments optimized, API calls

### Slide 8 — Roadmap & KPIs
- 0–90 days: GHL MarketOps, Scheduling, note draft + ICD/CPT, Stripe, Tebra
- 3–6 mo: Epic light, Patient360, no-show prediction, claim anomaly detection
- KPIs: note time -40%, coding >95%, show rate +10 pts, first-pass claims +8 pts

### Slide 9 — Go-To-Market & Moat
- GTM: GHL agencies + RCM partners; pilot 4–6 clinics for proof metrics
- Moat: native GHL operational AI, predictive compliance, federated learning
- End-to-end lifecycle: Marketing → Scheduling → EHR → Billing → Retention
