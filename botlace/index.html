Botlace is the world’s first AI‑SaaS operating system for healthcare delivery. The platform continuously adapts to each clinic’s specialty and workflows, automates documentation and charting, and learns from aggregate outcomes while preserving patient privacy.

Core AI SaaS Architecture

Three layers: AI Operating Layer (ACRE), Data & Integration Mesh, and SaaS Fabric.

AI Operating Layer (ACRE)

  • Medical‑grade reasoning trained on FHIR & evidence‑based standards
  • Adaptive decision intelligence per specialty
  • Auto‑generated clinical summaries & chart notes

Data & Integration Mesh

  • Secure FHIR APIs for Epic, ICANotes, Tebra
  • Federated learning without exposing PHI
  • AI‑driven auditing for HIPAA compliance

SaaS Fabric

  • Multitenant microservices with strong tenancy boundaries
  • Zero‑touch provisioning for instant onboarding
  • Modular: ClinicalOps, RevenueOps, MarketOps
